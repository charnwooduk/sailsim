On a touchscreen

Drag the wheel and the engine lever with a thumb. The arrows beside them are the bow and stern thrusters — hold them, they run only while pressed. The strip above handles the mooring lines: tap the name to change which line you are working, THROW sends it to the nearest bollard, or tap the bollard you want on the chart. ALL FAST is the same thing as M: bow, spring and stern together on the face she is lying against.

Drag the chart to pan, pinch to zoom, double-tap the water to follow her again. ☰ opens the drill, the weather and the boat; ⚓ opens the mooring lines; the numbers along the top open wind and tide. Tap this card to close it. A keyboard still works if one is attached.
